# Strategy summary (website & marketing proposal, 30 Oct 2026)

**Positioning:** a sunny chalet for 12 in the old village of La Rosière: a wood-fired hot tub facing the peaks, the lifts 300 m away, France and Italy on the same skis. Specific and true; no "luxe".

**Audience:** school-holiday family groups (FR, NL, DE, UK), friends' groups (UK, FR, NL) in January and March, multigenerational families at Christmas and in summer, summer hikers.

**Key messages:** 1) the terrace and hot tub; 2) room for 12 together (cathedral-ceiling living room, fireplace, sauna, 5 bedrooms); 3) easy skiing (300 m, ski room, 156 km shared with La Thuile).

**Website:** essentials → trust → booking on the front page; seasonal hero; FR + EN at launch, DE + NL after native review; Hostaway as the only booking path; registration number on every page once issued; no slogan.

**Design:** larch wood, slate, snow light, low sun. Palette `#F6F3EE` `#2A2622` `#2E5E86` `#B98A4E` `#6E7072` `#E2A23B`; Literata + Inter.

**Marketing:** Google first (Search Console, Google Vacation Rentals, chalet name in every platform title), aligned platform listings, reviews from every guest, Instagram, local partners, repeat-guest emails each September, short stays to fill January/March and summer.
